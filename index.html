What is Sonic Balance?

Sonic Balance™ is a revolutionary therapeutic system that combines cutting-edge heart rate variability (HRV) monitoring with precision vibroacoustic sound therapy. Unlike generic relaxation protocols, every session is personalized to YOUR nervous system's unique resonant frequency.

Using advanced biofeedback technology and a custom-designed treatment table, we identify the exact frequency where your sympathetic (fight-or-flight) and parasympathetic (rest-and-digest) nervous systems achieve balance—your personal homeostasis point.

Once identified, therapeutic sound and vibration are delivered directly through the body via specialized transducers positioned along acupuncture meridians. This creates a profound state of nervous system regulation that supports natural healing, stress reduction, and emotional balance.

The result: Deep physiological rest that allows your body to shift from survival mode to healing mode.

What to Expect - Your First Session

Before You Arrive

You'll receive intake forms and a brief questionnaire about your health history, current stress patterns, and healing goals.

Session Overview (90 minutes)

  • Consultation (15 min): We discuss your health concerns and what brought you to Sonic Balance
  • Baseline HRV Assessment (10 min): You'll wear a comfortable chest strap heart monitor while we establish your nervous system baseline
  • Frequency Discovery (30 min): You lie comfortably on our treatment table while we systematically test therapeutic frequencies and monitor your real-time physiological response
  • Personalized Treatment (30 min): Once we identify your homeostasis frequency, you'll receive therapeutic sound and vibration at that specific frequency
  • Integration (5 min): We review your session data, discuss what we observed, and answer questions

After Your Session

You'll receive a detailed PDF report showing your autonomic nervous system patterns, identified frequencies, and personalized recommendations. Most clients report feeling deeply relaxed, with effects that deepen over the following 24-48 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a session feel like?

Most clients describe Sonic Balance as deeply relaxing—similar to meditation but more profound. You'll feel gentle vibration through the treatment table while listening to therapeutic soundscapes. Many people enter a state between waking and sleeping, which is ideal for nervous system reset.

How is this different from regular massage or meditation?

Unlike massage, which works on muscles, or meditation, which requires mental effort, Sonic Balance directly influences your autonomic nervous system through precise vibroacoustic stimulation. We're addressing the physiological patterns that keep you stuck in stress responses, not just the symptoms.

How many sessions will I need?

Most clients notice shifts within 1-3 sessions, but lasting nervous system repatterning typically requires 6-10 sessions. We'll assess your progress and make recommendations based on your individual response.

Is this safe? Are there any side effects?

Sonic Balance is non-invasive and generally very safe. The most common "side effect" is deep relaxation that may last several hours. We do not recommend sessions for those with pacemakers, active psychosis, or in the first trimester of pregnancy. Please disclose all health conditions during intake.

Do you accept insurance?

We do not currently accept insurance, but we can provide detailed receipts for HSA/FSA reimbursement or out-of-network claims. Many clients find their insurance will cover a portion when submitted with proper documentation.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing. You'll be lying down for most of the session. Remove shoes, jewelry, and anything restrictive around the chest (for the heart monitor).
